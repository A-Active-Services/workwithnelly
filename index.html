Before You Tour Homes

KNOW YOUR THREE NUMBERS.

Before you begin touring homes, make sure you understand these three important numbers:

01
Your comfortable monthly payment
02
Your estimated cash to close
03
Your realistic purchase-price range

The amount you qualify to borrow is not always the same as the payment you will feel comfortable making every month.

Your total housing payment may include principal, interest, property taxes, homeowners insurance, mortgage insurance and HOA fees. Your cash to close may also include more than just the down payment.

A mortgage review can help you understand the complete picture before you fall in love with a property.
